Our Sacred Community

Sacred Heart Church Kota Sentosa is a vibrant Catholic parish located in Kuching, Sarawak. We are a community dedicated to faith, fellowship, and service.

Guided by our vision and mission, we strive to be disciples of the Word, working together in communion to fulfill our mission of spreading love and faith in our daily lives.

Our Vision

"A parish family rooted in Christ’s love, empowered by the Holy Spirit to lead, serve and proclaim the Gospel."

Our Mission

  • Courageous & Wise in Servant Leadership Leading with both courage and wisdom, in service to God and others.
  • Ardent in Worship Striving for Excellence in Liturgy.
  • Passionate in Witness Sharing the Love of Christ with fervour and commitment.
  • Deepening in Faith Cultivating a faith that continually seeks understanding and spiritual growth.

Reverence in the House of God

Mass Etiquette & Attire

To maintain the sanctity of the Holy Mass, we kindly request all parishioners and visitors to observe proper church etiquette and dress modestly.

Proper Attire

  • Do wear modest clothing that covers shoulders and knees.
  • Do wear collared shirts, blouses, trousers, or modest dresses.
  • Avoid sleeveless tops, shorts, mini-skirts, or revealing outfits.
  • Avoid slippers or flip-flops; please wear proper footwear.

Reverence & Participation

  • Arrive early: Aim to be seated 10-15 minutes before Mass begins to pray.
  • Genuflect: Bend your right knee towards the Tabernacle before entering your pew.
  • Fast: Observe the Eucharistic fast 1 hour before receiving Holy Communion.
  • Participate: Join warmly in the singing and spoken responses.

Distractions to Avoid

  • No phones: Please turn off or silence your mobile devices. No texting during Mass.
  • No food or drink: Chewing gum and snacks are strictly prohibited (water for medical reasons is allowed).
  • No chatting: Keep unnecessary conversations for outside the church after Mass.
  • Don't leave early: Please stay in your pew until the priest gives the final blessing and processes out.
