Guidelines to Arrange a Catholic Funeral

In times of loss, our parish is here to support you. Please read through the following guidelines carefully before submitting the arrangement form.

1. Roman Catholic Undertaker & Funeral Services

The Catholic Mutual Benefit Society (CMBS) of the Kuching Archdiocese provides assistance to bereaved families at the time of death.

Their services include providing advice regarding Christian rituals, cemetery plots, columbarium, caskets, transportation, arrangement for funeral masses, wake prayers for the dead, etc.

For more information regarding membership or in time of need / bereavement, Catholics are advised to contact the following persons for assistance to avoid unnecessary hassle and confusion with regards to Catholic funeral rites and services:


  • On-call (24/7): Christopher Chin (HP: 019-8563438)
  • CMBS Office: Beatrice Mary Goh (HP: 082-244493)

2. Memorial Monument Contractors

Only the following 2 contractors registered with the Kuching Catholic Cemeteries & Columbaria Services are authorized to erect and/or repair the monuments of your loved ones in our Catholic cemeteries.

  • Albert Sim Soon Chai: (HP: 016-8899344)
  • Simon Sim See Huat: (HP: 013-8117616)
