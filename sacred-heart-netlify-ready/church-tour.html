Infrastructure Highlights

Look a little closer at the details of our church, from its distinctive exterior to the heart of the celebration inside.

01 / Architecture

The Church Exterior

The pitched roof and prominent cross give the church its distinctive silhouette. The cross on the façade points to the faith at the centre of parish life.

See in the 3D illustration ↗
02 / Gathering

The Nave & Central Aisle

The nave is the main gathering space for the congregation. Rows of seating face the sanctuary, while the central aisle provides a route for liturgical processions.

See in the 3D illustration ↗
03 / Celebration

The Sanctuary & Altar

The sanctuary is the focal point of the worship space. The altar stands before a stone-faced backdrop, drawing the congregation’s attention to the celebration of the Eucharist.

See in the 3D illustration ↗
04 / Sacred details

The Tabernacle & Ambo

The tabernacle, set into the sanctuary backdrop, houses the reserved Blessed Sacrament. Beside the altar, the ambo is the place from which Scripture is proclaimed.
